## 2. Purpose & Principles
Replaces traditional milling with counter-current moving-bed cane diffuser. Shredded cane is leached with hot water ($75–85^\circ\text{C}$) and recirculated press water, achieving high extraction ($97.5–98.5\%$) with lower electrical drive power. Wet diffuser bagasse is dewatered through 2 dewatering mills.
