## 2. Principles
Condenses exhaust vapours from vacuum pans and last effect evaporators using cold injection water. Maintains operating vacuum ($12–20\text{ kPa abs}$) and removes non-condensable gases (air, $\text{CO}_2$, $\text{NH}_3$).
