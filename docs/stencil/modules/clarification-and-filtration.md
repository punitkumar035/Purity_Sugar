## 2. Purpose & Principles
Neutralizes organic acids in mixed juice with milk of lime ($\text{Ca(OH)}_2$) to pH $7.2–7.6$. Heated juice flashes non-condensable air, flocculates insoluble calcium phosphates, and settles in continuous tray/short-retention clarifiers (SRT). Clear juice overflows to evaporators; underflow mud is filtered on rotary vacuum drum filters (RVF) with bagacillo to recover sucrose as sweet water.
